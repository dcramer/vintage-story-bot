# Getting started: basics of playing the game

Survival baseline for the first five in-game days. Source for core goal definitions; quantities are targets, not guarantees. Recipes/ratios: in-game Handbook (`recipes` tool), not this file.

## Mechanics and rules

| Topic | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Hunger | Sprint costs more hunger than walking; use sparingly. Sitting reduces hunger drain (indoors at night only). Eat only until the hunger bar is half full; respawn returns half a bar, so nothing extra is wasted on death. |
| Death | Expected and recoverable (respawn). Death never ends a run; treat it as a cost, not a failure. |
| Night | Hostile mobs (drifters/shivers) spawn in darkness. Stay enclosed from sunset until 05:00. Dawn music can start ~03:00; use the clock, not music/light. Do not sleep; night is for indoor crafting. |
| Sneak | Approach or retreat from animals while sneaking. |
| Torches | Extinguish after 48 in-game hours. Pick up and re-place daily (morning) to reset. Two torches, one per end wall, maximize interior light and reduce spawns nearby. |
| Fuel | Ferns, firewood, peat all burn. |
| Map | Mark camp/house, clay, peat, and resin-leaking pine (do not chop) on the world map. |
| Clay order | Form storage vessel → pot → bowl so voxel usage never strands the large item. |
| Pit kiln safety | Never adjacent to grass, trees, or the house. Rammed earth does not burn. |
| Hides | Oil each medium/large hide with 1 fat immediately; store on floor. Cured when darkened (~1 day). |
| Storage vessel | Veggies, grain, mushrooms go in the vessel inside the sealed house (cellar rating) for winter. |
| Logs | Keep logs as logs when trees are scarce; fence consumes them. Place fence sparingly. |
| Spears | Thrown spears are picked up by walking over them. Carry 4–5. |
| Multi-use structures | Kiln covers and the A-frame roof double as lookout platforms and cover. |

## House spec

Internal 8×5, foundation 10×7, rammed earth (dirt → packed dirt → rammed earth). Shiver-proof even with roof gaps; hay-bale door seals it as a cellar.

1. Walls: 10×7 rectangle, 2 high. Leave a 1-wide door gap centered on a long wall; close with 2 hay bales stacked.
2. Narrow end walls (7 wide) rise to a gable: heights per column `2,2,3,4,3,2,2`.
3. Torch + firestarter before sunset; place torch on a wall once enclosed.
4. Once enclosed, dig the interior floor down 1 layer (that is the floor).
5. Roof: A-frame spanning the long axis following the gable profile. Gaps are acceptable.

Materials: rammed earth ≈ 2×(10+7)×2 walls + 8 gable + ~40 roof; 2 hay bales (16 grass).

## Pit kiln spec

- Layout: dig a 1-block pit; surround it with rammed earth in a plus (N/S/E/W of the pit, corners open).
- Load: place the raw pottery in the pit, then stack on top in order: 10 dry grass, 8 sticks, then 4 fuel (peat or firewood; storage vessel needs 8).
- Fire: ignite with a firestarter/torch and wait for completion before removing. Contact burns.
- Capacity: 1 storage vessel, or 4 small items (pot/bowl/jug/can/crock) per kiln.
- Chain kilns to share plus-blocks. Full cover ~28 rammed earth per kiln is optional; it prevents rain/wind extinguish and fire spread.
- Build 1–2 on day 2; no more until needed.

## Hunting

| Target | Method |
| --- | --- |
| Bird, raccoon | Days 2–3. Spear or club; low risk. Keep everything from dead birds. |
| Deer, goat | Throw spear from range; let it run and calm; repeat. |
| Boar | Spear once, switch to club; if it flees, throw spear again. |

## Day plans

Each day: leave at 05:00, return before sunset, reset torches, forage berries/mushrooms on the return path, eat berries to half bar.

### Day 1

- Knap 1 knife, 1 axe.
- 44 cattails → 2 handbaskets (10 each), 1 chest (24).
- Site: flat, level ground near a lake with cattails; not required to be ideal. Place chest, mark map. Chest: flint, berries, veggies, fat. Ground stacks: spare cattails, flax, bones, seeds, sticks, stones.
- Gather: 3–9 mushrooms, 8–15 berries, dead birds, 3 sticks (2 torches, shovel, backup knife), 40 clay (pot 4, bowl 1, vessel 35), 16 grass (2 hay bales).
- Build house (spec above) before dark.
- Night: organize inventories; second torch on opposite end wall; clayform vessel → pot → bowl; cook poultry; keep veggies; eat to half bar only.

### Day 2

- Build 1–2 pit kilns.
- Chop 1 tree (strip leaves first: less axe wear, more sticks/seeds).
- Cattails: 1 chest + 1 handbasket.
- ~30 clay (watering can, jug).
- Hunt 1 small animal.
- Seeds from wild crops. Medium-fertility soil into a chest. 5–9 peat.
- Night: clayform watering can, jug, crock; club (log + knife); pan (log + knife); replace worn stone tools; straw hat; backup torch.

### Day 3

- Plant seeds grouped by nutrient (N/P/K) roughly; layout is provisional.
- Cattails: 4th handbasket, 3rd chest. Spare cattails + horsetail → poultices.
- Fire kilns: vessel first; small items 4 per kiln.
- Hunt 1 animal.
- Night: reorganize; craft rough-hewn fence; 4–5 spears; 4 crocks (×4 recipe) if clay allows; improvised armor; cook meat only if starving, else wait for the pot.

### Day 4

- Swap kilns: pull fired, load raw; fired pottery goes indoors.
- Hunt deer, goat, or boar.
- Night: redmeat stew in the pot; oil hides; eat 1 bowl, pour rest into a crock; carry a bowl of food tomorrow; veggies/grain/mushrooms into the storage vessel.

### Day 5

- Expand garden: medium-fertility soil, till, water, plant new seeds.
- Fence the farm; leave a gate gap.
- Hunt 1 animal. Check kilns.
- Restock sticks, logs, fuel. Mark resin pines.
- Explore beyond the home radius.

After day 5: late stone age (pottery, no copper). Next tier is copper → bronze; farming and hunting continue as recurring goals.
